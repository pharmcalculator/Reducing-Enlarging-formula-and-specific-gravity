📚 How to Use This Pharmacy Quiz App

Welcome! This app helps you practice Reducing/Enlarging Formulas and Specific Gravity / Density calculations. Here's how it works:

  • 1 Filter questions — Use the dropdowns to pick a Chapter (Formula or SG) and a Difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard).
  • 2 Answer a question —
    • Multiple Choice: Click your chosen option. The app will highlight it green (correct) or red (wrong) and show the right answer.
    • Fill in the Blank: Type your answer in the box and press Enter or click Submit. It accepts both text and numbers (case-insensitive).
  • 3 Learn from feedback — After each answer, you'll see an explanation to reinforce the concept.
  • 4 Track your performance — The dashboard shows Attempted, Correct, and your Score % in real time.
  • 5 Navigate — Use ⟳ New Question for a random question, Next Question → to move forward, or 🔄 Reset Performance to start your stats fresh.
